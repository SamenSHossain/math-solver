# Coaching tendency scores

Scores are 0-100 with 50 = league-average head coach. `final` blends the research score (interviews, pressers, analysts, beat writers, Reddit/X/YouTube discussion found through web search) with a play-by-play metric score (five seasons of games attributed to the coach via the tenure table), weighted by research confidence and games coached. Six 2026-27 first-year coaches (Splitter, May, Mosley-NOP, Sweeney, Nori, Jenkins-MIL) carry little or no in-tenure data for their current team; Jenkins and Mosley inherit their prior-team metrics.

Research coverage caveat: the web-search budget available during the research pass was exhausted part-way, so several coaches (notably Rajakovic, Ott, Bickerstaff, Mazzulla, Carlisle) have low-confidence research dimensions; their final scores lean on the data metrics. `research_confidence` is the mean per-dimension confidence (0-1).

## Scheme tags and notes

**Mark Daigneault (OKC)** · defense: `switch,aggressive-help,blitz,ice,no-middle,zone` · offense: `five-out,drive-and-kick,heliocentric,pace-and-space,transition,midrange-hunting`
- Strengths: League's best and most aggressive defense: ball pressure, switching, turnover creation (No. 1 DRtg 2024-25 and early 2025-26) | Elite trust in and development of young players; deep, flexible rotations | Data-driven decision-making (challenge point system, 3PA philosophy) and strong in-series adjustments
- Criticisms: Late-game execution lapses in big spots (2024 DAL series, 2025 DEN series, 2026 home Game 7 loss to SAS) | Over-experimentation and unpredictable regular-season rotations can leave players without defined roles | Failed late challenges have cost timeouts in close playoff games

**Erik Spoelstra (MIA)** · defense: `zone,switch,aggressive-help,no-middle,blitz,drop` · offense: `dribble-handoff,five-out,motion,transition,pace-and-space,elbow-hub-big`
- Strengths: Elite scheme adaptability: league's heaviest zone user, switches coverages mid-series, reinvented offense around pace in 2025-26 | Best-in-class after-timeout and inbound play design; consistently praised playoff tactician (2020, 2023 Finals as low seeds) | Unmatched development of undrafted and young players (Robinson, Vincent, Strus, Martin, Adebayo, Herro)
- Criticisms: Late-game clock/timeout lapses (Nov 2024 timeout Miami did not have vs DET; 2024-25 clutch collapses during a 10-game losing streak) | Stingy and relatively unsuccessful with coach's challenges (fewest in NBA, 55.6% in 2024-25) | Constant lineup/rotation tinkering can leave roles unclear; 2024-25 and 2025-26 Heat underachieved relative to reputation

**Tyronn Lue (LAC)** · defense: `drop,stack-drop,switch,zone,blitz,aggressive-help` · offense: `pick-and-roll-heavy,heliocentric,isolation,slow-pace,dribble-handoff,post-centric-spot`
- Strengths: Best-in-class in-game and in-series adjustments by GM consensus; elite ATO play designer dating to the 2016 Finals. | Strong relationships with stars and locker-room credibility; voted top-two motivator. | Flexible defensive toolbox (drop with Zubac, switch/trap vs elite guards, situational zone).
- Criticisms: Slow, isolation-heavy half-court offense; team ranked near the bottom in pace and was called 'stagnant' by its own players (2025-26). | Constant lineup tinkering and late benchings of stars (Leonard publicly objected) can undermine rhythm. | No playoff series win since 2021 despite 50-win rosters; play-in exits in 2022 and 2026.

**Rick Carlisle (IND)** · defense: `aggressive-help,full-court-pressure,switch,drop,zone` · offense: `pace-and-space,transition,motion,five-out,egalitarian,dribble-handoff,early-offense`
- Strengths: Architect of the league's fastest, most egalitarian pass-and-pace offense (led NBA in pace, points and assists 2023-24) | Deep 10-11 man rotations that sustain tempo; bench units as weapons | Elite late-game poise: historic comebacks and buzzer-beaters throughout the 2025 run; 2011 title adjustments
- Criticisms: Friction with young stars over offensive control (Doncic in Dallas); slow to trust some young players (Mathurin, Walker) | Pace-at-all-costs system can produce leaky transition defense and low offensive rebounding | Two losing rebuild seasons (2021-23) and a bottom-of-the-East 2025-26 without Haliburton

**Mike Brown (NYK)** · defense: `drop,switch,zone,box-and-one,at-the-level,hedge,no-middle` · offense: `pace-and-space,dribble-handoff,motion,five-out,transition,0.5-second-decisions,high-3PA-volume`
- Strengths: 2026 NBA champion and two-time Coach of the Year; proven ability to modernize an offense quickly (Knicks 28th to 1st in 3PA rate; record Kings ORtg). | Multi-scheme defense (drop, at-the-level, switch, zone, box-and-one) with a strong defensive coordinator structure. | Deeper, more balanced rotations than his predecessor, keeping stars fresher for the playoffs.
- Criticisms: Sacramento tenure ended with a bottom-half defense, repeated officiating fines ($50K Jan 2024, $35K Nov 2024) and a firing after five straight one-possession losses. | Knicks players were reported midseason (Jan 2026) as still not adjusting to his tactics; Towns struggled with his new offensive and defensive roles. | Bench lineups without a primary ball handler and lineup experimentation drew criticism during the 2026 playoffs.

**Steve Kerr (GSW)** · defense: `switch,drop,zone,aggressive-help` · offense: `motion,split-cuts,dribble-handoff,five-out,pace-and-space,read-and-react,off-ball-screening`
