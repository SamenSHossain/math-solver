Proof of (A) Kőnig (Solutions/Sol_OAI_SeymourSecondNeighborhood_Pruning_konig.lean, CHECK OK), deficiency form of Hall's theorem. Let A₀ := E.image Prod.fst and N(S) := {b : ∃ (a,b) ∈ E, a ∈ S} (nbr). Choose S ⊆ A₀ maximising the deficiency |S| − |N(S)| (truncated subtraction), normalised so that |N(S)| ≤ |S| (if the maximiser has |N(S)| > |S| the maximum is 0 and S = ∅ works); let δ := |S| − |N(S)|. Augment the right side by δ dummy vertices: for a ∈ A₀ put t(a) := N({a}).disjSum (univ : Finset (Fin δ)) ⊆ B ⊕ Fin δ. Hall's condition holds: for a nonempty s ⊆ A₀ with image S' ⊆ A₀, N(S').disjSum univ ⊆ s.biUnion t, so |s| = |S'| ≤ |N(S')| + δ ≤ |s.biUnion t| by maximality of δ. Mathlib's Finset.all_card_le_biUnion_card_iff_exists_injective gives an injective f : A₀ → B ⊕ Fin δ with f a ∈ t a. Let M := E.filter (fun e => ∃ h : e.1 ∈ A₀, f ⟨e.1, h⟩ = inl e.2). M ⊆ E; Prod.fst is injective on M (f ⟨a,_⟩ determines b) and Prod.snd is injective on M (injectivity of f). Counting: univ.image f ⊆ M.image (inl ∘ snd) ∪ univ.image inr, so |A₀| = |univ.image f| ≤ |M| + δ, hence |A₀| ≤ k + δ by hypothesis hk. The cover is KA := A₀ \ S, KB := N(S): an edge (a,b) ∈ E has a ∈ A₀, and either a ∉ S (so a ∈ KA) or a ∈ S (so b ∈ N(S)); its size is |A₀| − |S| + |N(S)| = |A₀| − δ ≤ k.
